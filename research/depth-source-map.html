Textbook revision: sources and evidence

The September 2026 depth revision develops all eleven chapters with derivations, operating examples, counterexamples, and worked solutions. The editorial benchmark is the stronger Stanford AA203 study chapters and CMU AI Agents study chapters. Their teaching methods informed the revision; their subject matter and distinctive prose were not copied.

This is a paper-based research textbook. No verified Khan lecture transcript was available. The transcript-grounded-textbook workflow was adapted to papers and explicit teaching constructions; there are no invented lecture timestamps. The section-level map records every chapter section, its adjacent citations, and this evidence boundary. The claim-level source ledger supplies primary-source locators and publication status.

Review standard

The representative foundations chapter and project were completed and checked before expansion. The full revision then received a same-agent scientific/editorial pass: definitions and assumptions before claims, consequential derivation steps, numerical or limiting-case checks, source attribution, and explicit model boundaries. Quantitative parity counts exclude navigation and use the same counter on old, revised, and reference chapters. Counts diagnose omissions; they do not establish correctness or replace expert peer review.
