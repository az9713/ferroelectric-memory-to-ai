Coverage limits

This is a verified bounded baseline, not an author-certified exhaustive bibliography. OpenAlex omitted several 2026 works; arXiv and targeted publisher searches repaired known gaps. Unindexed proceedings, invited abstracts, newly accepted manuscripts, and patents may remain missing. DOI dates are publisher-deposited and can represent event, online, or issue dates. Each field retains its provenance; dates with only a year or month are not assigned fictional days. Source access and metadata failures are logged.

The inventory distinguishes records assessed through full-text sections from metadata-only records. For the latter, title-level scope is provided but contributions and quantitative limitations remain unassessed; venue status does not substitute for scientific review. The course develops representative mechanisms deeply rather than pretending all 101 records were critically reviewed.
