About this research course

This independent educational course examines Asif Islam Khan's research at Georgia Tech from materials physics through devices, memory, digital implementation, AI workloads, and facility energy. It is not an official university course or a product endorsement.

The source baseline covers 26 September 2021–26 September 2026. It contains 101 deduplicated scholarly records: 14 selected full-text assessments and 87 explicitly metadata-only records. Publication types, version links, uncertain matches, and coverage limits remain visible. Original papers are linked to publishers and repositories; downloaded third-party source PDFs are excluded from this public edition.

What the course contains

Ten chapters and a cumulative capstone provide approximately 19,600 teaching words, 41 expandable exercise solutions, additional worked examples, a prerequisite map, guided readings, a glossary, and runnable Python/SPICE/Verilog projects. Source equations, teaching assumptions, and research claims are distinguished. Download the repository to run the code; GitHub Pages serves the documents and saved results.

Limits of the models

The SPICE work is an illustrative teaching model including reproduction of published static equations. It is not a complete author-model reproduction or calibrated experimental-device reconstruction. Static loops do not establish measured switching dynamics, retention, endurance, temperature response, or full-access energy. The capstone's material-to-window and energy couplings are explicit synthetic assumptions.

The physical views are conceptual diagrams and uncharacterized macro abstractions. No compatible research-device PDK, foundry rules, extraction decks, or characterized macro were available. Generic controller synthesis does not establish detailed placement/routing, timing closure, foundry DRC/LVS/PEX, or a fabrication-ready memory. Corporate affiliations establish research collaboration, not deployed product integration.

Source date and update policy

This GitHub Pages site is a static, dated publication. It does not offer an automatic weekly research-monitoring service. New papers, revisions, corrections, and code releases need primary-source verification and scientific review before they are incorporated. A newly discovered older work must not be presented as a new finding. The baseline had three inaccessible laboratory/center sources; unindexed works and unreleased data may remain missing.

Authorship procedures and dependencies

Scientific authorship used rigorous-explainer; practical documentation used technical-docs-writer; scientific editing used science-editor. Transcript-grounded-textbook was inspected, but no verified lecture transcript was available, so teaching is paper-based. No model switch or independent review is claimed.

Verified calculation environment: Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.16.0, Matplotlib 3.9.3, ngspice 47, Icarus Verilog 12, and Yosys 0.33. Mathematics uses locally bundled MathJax 3.2.2 with its Apache license. Third-party publications retain their original rights.
