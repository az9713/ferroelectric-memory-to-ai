Prof. Asif Khan and collaborators: research bibliography

This bibliography contains only works with Prof. Asif Khan in the verified author list. It excludes the course's general semiconductor, computer-systems, software, and energy references. The first section identifies the 15 selected papers and preprints developed in the connected research synthesis. The second preserves the other 87 records from the five-year inventory, including one paper used briefly for comparison.

The total is 102 distinct works: 101 records from 26 September 2021 through 26 September 2026, plus one foundational June 2021 paper. This is a bounded bibliography, not a complete career publication list. Versions of the same work and supplements are not counted again. Where a journal publication and a preprint have different titles or dates, the record identifies the version used. Preprints are explicitly distinguished from journal articles.

The 14 selected in-window assessments concern relevant full-text sections, not exhaustive verification of every result or reference. The 2022 empirical compact-model discussion is supported by the corresponding Wang thesis section; the thesis is not counted as a separate coauthored paper. The 87 other records retain their metadata-only assessment status. Displayed author names use “Prof. Asif Khan” consistently; the original linked publications retain their published author forms.
