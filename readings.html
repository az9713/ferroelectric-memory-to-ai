Guided paper readings

Read selectively with a question, then inspect the methods and figures needed to answer it. The guides below concern acquired sources. Answers distinguish what a paper establishes from what the course derives or assumes. A PDF being saved does not mean every reference and supplement has been audited.

1. Extracting a model from a loop

Wang et al., Extraction of Preisach model parameters for fluorite-structure ferroelectrics and antiferroelectrics, 2021; foundational, outside the five-year window. Read after Chapters 1–3. Start with PDF p. 2 equations 1–4, then p. 3 Figure 2 and the supplement.

Before reading, predict why the displacement zero crossing might differ from the polarization coercive field. During reading, label the ferroelectric and linear dielectric terms. After reading, reproduce Figure 2's stated synthetic parameters rather than treating the curve as measured data.

Reading solution

Displacement includes the linear background response, so setting total displacement to zero need not set polarization to zero. The course computes approximately 0.88643 MV/cm for the positive displacement crossing although the polarization parameter is 1 MV/cm. The model's static loop does not provide a switching-time law. Saturation-only data leave combinations of polarization and offset poorly identified.

2. From capacitor equations to an actual FeFET

Zheng Wang thesis, PDF pp. 49–54, §3.3, related to TED 2022. Read after Chapters 3–4. Write the device geometry, read drain bias, program/erase pulses, and threshold-extraction criterion before interpreting its memory window.

Find where electrostatics alone fails and why the model introduces screening/leakage. Identify which parameters belong to the transistor, ferroelectric, interface, and empirical screening current. Ask whether every required input is numerically specified.

Reading solution

The source concerns a p-channel Si device with 5 nm ZrO₂ and 1.5 nm SiO₂; it is not the synthetic 10 nm Figure 2 capacitor. Screening changes the coupling between polarization and observed memory behavior. A complete reconstruction still needs baseline transistor calibration, full current prefactor, raw traces, and executable model details. Transplanting convenient parameters from the synthetic capacitor would create an unvalidated hybrid.

3. A domain is not a bit

Sub-nm² ferroelectric domains via charged 180° walls in ZrO₂, arXiv 2507.18920v2. Read after Chapter 2. Inspect the structural images and the explanation of head-to-head and tail-to-tail walls. Draw the bound-charge sign at each wall and identify what could compensate it.

Reading solution

A polarization discontinuity can create wall charge, requiring electrostatic compensation or another stabilizing mechanism. Atomic imaging plus modeling can establish unusual domain structures under the studied conditions. It does not demonstrate separate electrodes, selective switching, read discrimination, retention distributions, or integration of those domains as independently addressable bits.

4. Sensing determines architecture

A Full Spectrum of 3D Ferroelectric Memory Architectures Shaped by Polarization Sensing, arXiv 2504.09713. Read after Chapters 4–6. Sort architectures by what is sensed, whether reading disturbs state, what access devices and drivers are required, and what limits three-dimensional integration.

Reading solution

Different sensing choices move costs among cell, reference, sense amplifier, restore operation, and peripheral circuitry. A proposed architecture should be assessed as a complete access path. Device demonstrations and scaling proposals in a review do not share one evidence level. Treat a projected layer count or density as a scenario until array and integration conditions are established.

5. Reliability with matched conditions

Compare first-switch trap generation, thermal processing of IGO/IWO channels, and radiation response of laminated stacks. Read after Chapters 2, 4, and 8. For each, create a small table of stress, measured response, comparison group, extraction method, and extrapolation that would be unjustified.

Reading solution

The stresses are different physical experiments. First-switch damage concerns electrical history and interface-sensitive extraction. Thermal processing concerns survival after stated exposure and capping conditions. Radiation response concerns dose and bias/stack conditions. None alone establishes a universal endurance law or a qualified storage system. A fair comparison retains the precise failure criterion and stress protocol.

6. Interfacial charge as a mechanism claim

Interfacial-charge study, arXiv 2609.20482. Read after Chapters 2–4. Separate direct electrical/chemical observables from inferred defect populations and internal bias. Ask which alternative mechanism would fit the same read-window change and which additional measurement discriminates it.

Reading solution

Combining multiple observables and a physical model is stronger than interpreting one window shift. The explanation still depends on extraction assumptions and model identifiability. A useful extension would fit competing mechanisms jointly to reversal, temperature, and pulse-history data, then predict withheld conditions rather than only reducing the original residual.

7. AI models of device models

Compare retention surrogate, arXiv 2603.06881v3 with agentic TCAD, arXiv 2609.12184v2. Read after Chapters 3, 4, and 9. Record training/calibration data, held-out conditions, solver and version, runtime boundary, and whether experimental measurements or another simulator supply the target.

Reading solution

A surrogate approximates a reference mapping; an agentic workflow can help choose models and parameters. Accuracy against a simulator is not automatically accuracy against devices. Withheld geometry or bias is stronger evidence than a random split of adjacent points, but still does not establish generalization to new process physics. Corporate affiliations establish collaboration, not product integration. The proprietary Sentaurus dependency and incomplete released workflow prevent claiming independent reproduction here.

A reusable paper-assessment worksheet

  1. Identify title, version, publication status, and verified author match.
  2. State the intervention and exact observable, including units and conditions.
  3. Separate measurements, inferred mechanism, simulation, and projection.
  4. List comparison controls, uncertainty, and likely confounders.
  5. Reproduce one supported relation or calculation; retain its scope.
  6. Trace one upward consequence and one downward requirement.
  7. Name the missing measurement most likely to change the conclusion.
  8. Decide read now, later, or background based on the current question.

Read now: the extraction paper and the device-model thesis section. Read next: architecture and reliability sources after their prerequisites. Read the newest AI/defect preprints when you can challenge the assumptions, rather than equating recency with readiness.
